From forces to the differential equation

m y″ = −k y − D y′ + Fₑ(t)

Position y is measured from the unforced equilibrium, positive upward. The combined spring and gravitational force is −ky and linear viscous damping is −Dy′. Mass m > 0, stiffness k > 0 and damping D ≥ 0. Primes denote time derivatives. In the vertical model, mg = kN at equilibrium, so the constant gravity term cancels when displacement is measured from equilibrium.
